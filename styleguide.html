00

Principles

For two, not for one. Every screen belongs to both people equally — both avatars always visible, never "your list," always "our list."
Warm, never childish. Rounded and friendly but adult. Playful moments are reserved for shared wins — both check in "done."
One accent color. Mars orange is the only hue carrying meaning beyond labels and semantics. If it's not orange, it's warm neutral or semantic.

05

Motion

Reminder firing — badge pulses (1.2s ring) until answered.
now — done?
Modal — pop-in scale(.96→1) 160ms · Toast — slide-up 200ms (see 4.15).

Default curve cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1), 120–200ms. prefers-reduced-motion: disable pulse/glow, keep 150ms fades. Check-in celebration: badge pulses once, toast slides, card fades to completed 200ms.
